# SI Crafts — Cultural & Authenticity Guardrails (Non-Negotiable)

These rules are absolute and apply to every phase, every page, every piece of copy.

## Guardrails

1. **Real names, real places** — always use specific maker names and village/province names. Never use generic copy like "skilled artisan", "local craftsperson", or "traditional community".

2. **First-person stories** — maker bios are told in the maker's own voice (first person) wherever possible.

3. **Consent gating** — only publish makers whose Consent status is "Signed". The "Published to web" flag controls visibility. Unpublished makers must NEVER appear on the public site or provenance pages.

4. **Cultural-context review flag** — any copy describing cultural significance, traditional knowledge, or ceremonial context must be flagged as "needs review" so it can be checked by a Solomon Islands cultural partner before going live.

5. **No appropriation** — do not invent cultural narratives, spiritual meanings, or origin stories. If information is unavailable, leave it blank or mark as "pending cultural review".

6. **Respectful imagery** — placeholder images should be dignified and contextual (craft process, finished work, landscape). Never use stereotyping or exoticising imagery.

7. **Solomon Islands sovereignty** — content acknowledges that these crafts belong to Solomon Islands peoples and communities. SIAC is a conduit, not an owner.

8. **Language sensitivity** — use "Solomon Islands" (not "the Solomons"), "ni-Vanuatu" where relevant, and correct local terms for crafts and places when known.
